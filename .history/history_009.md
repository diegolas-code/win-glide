# win-glide Project History: Physics Feel Tuning

## Change: Physics Parameters Adjustment
**Decision:** Increased top speed and decreased acceleration.
**Reasoning:** 
- The user requested a faster peak movement speed but a longer "spin-up" time to reach that speed.
- This creates a more weighted, powerful feel where the window builds significant momentum over a slightly longer duration.

**Tuning Details:**
- **Acceleration:** Reduced from 10,000 to **6,000** pixels/s².
- **Top Speed:** Increased from 2,500 to **4,000** pixels/s.
- **Spin-up Time:** Now takes approximately **0.67 seconds** to reach top speed (was 0.25 seconds).
